Meet Deck · annonator

Your meeting media stays in your browser.

Meet Deck connects Google Meet and Stream Deck only over IPv4 loopback. No cloud, no telemetry, and no meeting media or communications.

  1. 1

    Install the Stream Deck plugin

    Install the open-source plugin and drag a Meet Deck action onto an LCD key.

  2. 2

    Open pairing

    Generate an eight-digit code in the Property Inspector. It expires quickly and works only on this computer.

  3. 3

    Connect the extension

    Open the Meet Deck popup, enter the code, and choose Pair and connect. Chrome may ask you to allow Meet Deck to reach only the configured WebSocket origin on 127.0.0.1.

What the extension processes

Locally derived

Visible control labels are inspected transiently. The local plugin receives only whether zero, one, or multiple meetings are active plus finite microphone, camera, hand, and own-presentation states.

Never sent to the plugin

No meeting URLs, codes, titles, names, chats, captions, audio, video, or screen data.
